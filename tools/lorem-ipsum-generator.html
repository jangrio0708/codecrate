What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled, pseudo-Latin filler text that has been the standard placeholder in the printing and design industry since the 1500s. Because it approximates the letter and word-length distribution of real language, it looks like readable text without distracting from a layout's visual design.

When to use it

Use Lorem Ipsum when mocking up a page layout, testing how a design handles varying amounts of text, or filling content areas in a wireframe before real copy is ready.
