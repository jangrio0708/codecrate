About CodeCrate

CodeCrate is a small set of free, browser-based developer tools — a JSON formatter, Base64 and URL encoders, a Lorem Ipsum generator, a case converter, a Markdown previewer, a color converter, and a UUID generator. No sign-up, no installation, and no data leaves your device: every tool runs entirely in JavaScript in your browser.

Why we built this

Developer tool sites are often slow, ad-heavy in the wrong way, or require creating an account just to format some JSON. CodeCrate is meant to be the opposite: open a tool, get your result, done.

How it's funded

CodeCrate is supported by display advertising, which keeps every tool free with no accounts or paywalls.
